Privacy policy

Road To has no accounts, no servers, and collects or shares no data. This page explains what the app stores and what never leaves your phone.

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

In short

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Your plan, your history, your streak, and your photos stay only on your phone.

What the app stores and where

Your plan, sessions, streak, settings, and reminders live in a local database inside the app. Progress photos are optional and stay in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to the internet: uninstall the app and everything is deleted.

Permissions and sensors

Camera: only requested if you tap "take photo", and the photo stays on your device. Notifications: local reminders you can turn off at any time. Proximity and accelerometer sensors: they count your reps on the phone itself, without recording audio or images.

What we do not do

We do not create accounts. We do not collect personal data. We do not use analytics or remote crash reporting. We do not show ads. We do not sell or share information with third parties. There is no server and no cloud.

Children

Road To is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your data

Everything lives on your device, so you control it: clear the app data in Android Settings or uninstall the app, and it is gone. We cannot see it or recover it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this same page with its date.
